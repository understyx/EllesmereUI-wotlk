# Micro Menu Port Notices

This sub-addon contains the following adapted material from DragonUI:

- `EllesmereUIBlizzardSkin_MicroMenu.lua`: DragonUI's micro-button atlas coordinates and the Modern state/background treatment are adapted from `DragonUI/modules/micromenu.lua`.
- `Media/MicroMenu/uimicromenu2x.blp`: copied from `DragonUI/Textures/Micromenu/uimicromenu2x.blp`. DragonUI's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` identifies this sheet as obtained via DragonflightUI.
- `Media/MicroMenu/micropvp.blp`: copied from `DragonUI/Textures/Micromenu/micropvp.blp`. DragonUI's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` identifies it as obtained via DragonflightUI.

The Lua module's attribution comment points to this notice. These are the only DragonUI textures copied. `DragonUI_Options/panel/tab_micromenu.lua` was consulted as a reference only. `DragonUI/Textures/UI/MicroMenu.blp` was inspected but is not used.

The micro-button atlas and PvP faction art are World of Warcraft artwork owned by Blizzard Entertainment. They are not covered by either MIT license below and are included only for use with World of Warcraft.
